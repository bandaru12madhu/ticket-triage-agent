# Billing questions

## Duplicate charges
A pending charge and a completed charge can look like a duplicate. Pending charges drop off within 3 business days. If both are still there after that, support can review the payment.

## Invoices and receipts
Receipts are emailed after every order. Past invoices can be downloaded from Orders in the account page.

## Payment methods
We accept major credit cards, debit cards and digital wallets. We do not accept checks or cash.

## Cancelling a subscription
A subscription can be cancelled any time from Settings, then Billing. Access continues until the end of the paid period and no refund is given for unused days.
