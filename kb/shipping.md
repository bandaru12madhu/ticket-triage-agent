# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed after 2 pm ship the next business day.

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show updates after the first scan.

## Changing an address
The delivery address can be changed only before the order ships. After that the customer must contact the carrier.

## Shipping costs
Orders over 50 dollars ship free with standard shipping. Express shipping always has a fee.
