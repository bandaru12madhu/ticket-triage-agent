# Refund policy

## Refund window
Items can be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging.

## Late or missing orders
If an order has not arrived 10 days after it shipped, the customer can ask for a full refund or a replacement.

## Damaged items
If an item arrives damaged, the customer should send a photo of the damage. We then refund the item price or send a replacement.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 7 business days to show up.

## Items that cannot be refunded
Gift cards and digital downloads cannot be refunded.
